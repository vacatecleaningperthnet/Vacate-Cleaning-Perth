Everything you need to know about vacate cleaning and getting your bond back in Perth.

Vacate cleaning — also known as end of lease cleaning or bond cleaning — is a detailed, checklist-driven clean performed when you move out of a rental property in Perth. It is significantly more thorough than a regular domestic clean because it must meet the standard expected by property managers and landlords during a final inspection. It covers every room, including ovens, rangehoods, grout lines, window tracks, skirting boards, and built-in wardrobes.

Yes, we stand behind our work with a genuine bond back guarantee. If your property manager identifies any cleaning issue related to our work within 5 business days of your final inspection, we return and fix it at absolutely no charge. This guarantee applies to the specific areas we cleaned — it does not cover damage, wear and tear, or pre-existing conditions that were present before your tenancy began.

The cost depends on the size of your property (number of bedrooms and bathrooms), its current condition, and any add-on services such as carpet steam cleaning. We provide firm, upfront quotes with no hidden fees. A typical one-bedroom apartment starts from around $250, while a four-bedroom house ranges from $480 to $650 depending on condition. Contact us for an accurate quote specific to your property.
